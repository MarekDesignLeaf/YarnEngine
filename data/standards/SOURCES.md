# Source Register

CYC_KNIT_SYMBOLS
Craft Yarn Council, Knit Chart Symbols
https://craftyarncouncil.com/standards/knit-chart-symbols
Purpose: seed vocabulary and definitions for common knitting operations and cable notation.

CYC_STANDARDS
Craft Yarn Council, Standards and Guidelines for Crochet and Knitting
https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/standards
Purpose: industry conventions for yarn, gauge, needles, abbreviations and symbols.

ISO_1144_2016
ISO 1144:2016, Textiles — Universal system for designating linear density (Tex System)
https://www.iso.org/standard/70774.html
Purpose: tex as canonical yarn linear-density unit.
Only public metadata is referenced. Copyrighted standard text is not reproduced.

ARBATAITIS_2021_PART_I
E. Arbataitis et al. Flexible Theoretical Calculation of Loop Length and Area Density of Weft-Knitted Structures: Part I.
Materials 2021, 14, 3059.
https://doi.org/10.3390/ma14113059
Purpose: published geometric equations for needle-loop and float lengths in weft-knitted structures.
Boundary: experimental work concerns machine-knitted fabrics; hand-knitting accuracy is not assumed.
