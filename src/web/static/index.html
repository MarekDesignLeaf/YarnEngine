      How it is calculated
      
    
      The exact count of every operation is derived from the pattern (or the amigurumi rounds). Without calibration, yarn per stitch is estimated from stitch geometry (gauge, yarn diameter, number of hook pull-throughs); single crochet is anchored to a published measurement (Storck et al. 2022). A measured swatch or an approved calibration model replaces the estimate with a measured figure.
